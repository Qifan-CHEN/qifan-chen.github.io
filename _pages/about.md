Hello, I am Chen Qifan, I am a Hedrick Math Fellow at UCLA, working with [Prof. Hayden Schaeffer](https://sites.google.com/view/haydenschaeffer/home) and Prof. [Yifan Chen](https://yifanc96.github.io/).

I received my PhD in 2026 at The Ohio State University, where I was advised by Prof. [Yulong Xing](https://people.math.osu.edu/xing.205/). I received my B.S. in Mathematics from The Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2021. You can find my biography [here](https://qifan-chen.github.io//biography/)  


My research interests lie in Numerical Analysis, Scientific Computing, and AI for Science. My thesis involves developing temporal discretizations for hyperbolic conservation laws. I also develop data-driven modeling of systems governed by unknown equations. I welcome discussions and potential collaborations.

Email: chen7fan@g.ucla.edu
