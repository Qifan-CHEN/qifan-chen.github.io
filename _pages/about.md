Hello, I am Chen Qifan, I am a Hedrick Math Fellow at UCLA, working with [Prof. Hayden Schaeffer](https://sites.google.com/view/haydenschaeffer/home) and Prof. [Yifan Chen](https://yifanc96.github.io/).


I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from The Ohio State University in 2026, advised by Prof. Yulong Xing. During my Ph.D., I also collaborated closely with Prof. Dongbin Xiu on data-driven modeling of dynamical systems, particularly flow-map learning. I received my B.S. in Mathematics from The Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2021. You can find my biography [here](https://qifan-chen.github.io//biography/)  

My research focuses on numerical methods for partial differential equations and data-driven modeling of dynamical systems. I am particularly interested in high-order numerical methods for hyperbolic conservation laws, reduced-order modeling, and scientific machine learning for PDEs.

I welcome research discussions and potential collaborations.
